# Dashavidha Pariksha - The Tenfold Ayurvedic Examination

## What It Is

Dashavidha Pariksha (literally "tenfold examination") is the classical Ayurvedic method of assessing the diseased person (atura / rogi) before treatment - described in **Charaka Samhita, Vimana Sthana, Adhyaya 8**. It is **person-focused** (unlike SOCRATES which is symptom-focused). The ten parameters jointly determine:
- **Rogi Bala** - patient (host) strength
- **Roga Bala** - disease strength
- **Ayu Bala** - lifespan quality/quantity

## The 10 Parameters

| # | Parameter | What It Assesses | Assessment Approach |
|---|-----------|------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Prakriti** | Innate constitution (Vata/Pitta/Kapha) at birth | Baseline to measure deviation from; indicates inherent strength |
| 2 | **Vikriti** | Current pathological deviation from Prakriti | Which doshas aggravated/lodged + where; the vitiation to treat |
| 3 | **Sara** | Tissue quality / essence / vitality | Strength of each dhatu (Rasa, Rakta, Mamsa, Meda, Asthi, Shukra, Oja); = immunity |
| 4 | **Samhanana** | Body compactness & integration | Tightness/firmness of tissues & joints; low compactness = more disease proneness |
| 5 | **Pramana** | Body measurements & proportions | Anguli (dimensions), Anjali (tissue quantity), Sankhya (organ count) |
| 6 | **Satmya** | Adaptability / lifestyle compatibility | Foods/activities/behaviors the body has adapted to over time |
| 7 | **Sattva** | Mental strength & fortitude | Graded Pravara (strong) / Madhyama (moderate) / Avara (weak) |
| 8 | **Ahara Shakti** | Digestive / metabolic capacity | Appetite, intake patterns, digestive efficiency, metabolic outcomes |
| 9 | **Vyayama Shakti** | Exercise capacity & endurance | Physical activity tolerance without fatigue/breathlessness |
| 10 | **Vaya** | Age & life-stage physiology | Contextualizes all other parameters for treatment intensity |

## Clinical Significance

- **Prakriti strength ranking:** Kapha-predominant = strong, Pitta = moderate, Vata = less strong.
- **Sara = immunity:** stronger dhatus = stronger person = better defense mechanism.
- **Samhanana:** lesser compactness = more prone to disease.
- **Sattva types:** Avara (weak) patients need more reassurance and gentler, more prolonged regimens; Pravara can tolerate aggressive treatment.
- **Ahara Shakti:** determines whether the gut can metabolize oral herbal medicines - critical for deita/pharmacy decisions.
- **Risk of miscalibration:** "Administering intensively potent medicines without skillfully judging the strength and tolerance of the patient may cause serious life-threatening complications and eventual death." Weak medicines in strong patients with serious disease fail to resolve it.

## Mapping to MediKiosk

- **Two-layer schema required:** SOCRATES (symptom, 8 fields) + Dashavidha (person, 10 parameters) co-exist - neither replaces the other.
- **Sattva -> UX accessibility:** low-Sattva patients need more audio prompts + simpler touch interfaces (aligns with the low-literacy requirement).
- **CCRAS Prakriti Assessment Scale (PAS)** is the modern standardized questionnaire used to assess Prakriti - a strong reference for building the AYUSH question battery.
- Each parameter needs a specific touchscreen question flow and audio prompt for self-service capture.
